HOW TO PLAY

STEALTH IS YOUR ONLY ADVANTAGE.

[ MOVEMENT ]

Use W A S D or the Arrow Keys to move around the computer lab.

[ HIDE ]

Press E to become hidden for 5 seconds. Use it to break a chase and reposition.

[ SABOTAGE ]

Stand beside a computer and stay there for 1.5 seconds. Each sabotage earns 1 point; a sabotaged computer resets after 15 seconds.

[ THE GUARD ]

A yellow detection cone shows the guard's vision. If detected, the guard chases you. Breaking line-of-sight or hiding can end the chase.

[ PAUSE ]

Press P or ESC, or use the on-screen button. While paused, the mission clock and guard stop.

[ RETURN ]

During a mission, choose BACK TO MENU to return to this terminal without leaving the website.
